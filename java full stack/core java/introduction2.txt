AI 
===
AI stands for Artificial Intelligence.
AI is a capability of a computer or a system to mimic or simulate human behaviours.
ex:
	Thinking 	
	Reasoning 
	Language understanding 	
	Perception 	
	and etc.
We have three types of AI.

1) Weak / Narrow AI 
------------------
	It is limited to narrow areas.
	ex:
		ChatGPT, Alexa, Siri, GoogleMaps and etc. 

2) Strong AI 
------------
	It is used to mimic human behaviours.
	ex:
		Not Yet Achieved

2) Super AI 
---------
	It is used to surpass human behaviours.
